Adapted by Peel Studio from Player 2's EVH Wolfgang neck-pickup direct
recordings. Changes: extracted individual notes, converted to mono PCM,
reduced background noise, aligned attacks, trimmed tails and applied fades
and a shared gain. Playback may change pitch, level and amp/cabinet tone.

Paper: Hegel Pedroza, Wallace Abreu, Ryan M. Corey and Iran R. Roman,
"Guitar-TECHS: An Electric Guitar Dataset Covering Techniques, Musical
Excerpts, Chords and Scales Using a Diverse Array of Hardware."
ICASSP 2025. https://guitar-techs.github.io/
